Step B

Bridge Curiosity

Spark interest before reading.

Example: Key Stage 3, Week 1, Session One

TutorClose your eyes. What comes to your mind when you hear or read the word “CLUES”?

Revisit Prior Knowledge

Immerse in Reading

Learners read. They do not just listen.

Show three sentencesfrom the upcoming passage

Step D

Discuss Meaning and Ideas

Example: Key Stage 3, Week 1

Session OneMatch meanings to underlined words. Circle the context clues.
Session TwoFind the root word, prefix and suffix.
Session ThreeWrite the literal and the implied meaning.
Session FourCompare print and online references.
Step G

Grow Vocabulary and Fluency

Use the new words.

Example: Key Stage 3, Week 1

  • Work out a word’s meaning from the clues
  • Read sentences with a partner
  • Write sentences with the new words
  • Read them aloud. The tutor flashes the day’s words.
Step E

Evaluate Understanding

Look back at what was learned.

Example: Key Stage 3, Week 1

One word, one clueWrite the word and the clue that helped
Smiley scale“How confident do I feel using context clues now?”
Reflection on the LASReflection activities on the LAS
Added rule

A tutor is not a classroom teacher

✕Not this

  • Lecturing while learners do the LAS
  • Giving the answer when a learner hesitates

✓This

  • Learners answer the LAS on their own
  • Help only when stuck: a question or a hint
Added rule

Match the guide to the learner

  • Check the Reading Profile: Phil-IRI and what you observe.
  • Too hard? Step down to the Basic guide.
  • Already strong? Move up to the Plus guide.
