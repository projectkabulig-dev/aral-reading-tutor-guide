I Do, We Do, You Do

Example: the letter Mm, Week 2

1I Do Tutor shows the letter Mm and models it
2We Do Tutor and learners say the name and sound together
3You Do Learners say it one by one, and write it in the air

Name, sound and form

Do not skip the presentation.

Stress name, sound and formRepeat as a class, then one by one
Do not skip phoneme manipulationChildren must know the sounds, not just memorize
Build words and non-wordsLearners point out words they do not know

Use the learners’ language

If learners cannot follow the instructions

1Say the instruction in English
2Translate it into the learners’ language
3Say it again in English

Support struggling learners

Growth mindset“Mistakes help your brain grow.”
Positive self-talk“Reading is hard, but I can do it.”

Added rule

A tutor is not a classroom teacher

✕Not this

  • Lecturing during You Do and workbook practice
  • Giving the answer when a learner hesitates

✓This

  • Learners answer on their own
  • Help only when stuck: a question or a hint
Added rule

Match the guide to the learner

  • Check the Reading Profile: Phil-IRI and what you observe.
  • Struggling? Stay in KS1 and use the review sessions.
  • Strong? Move up to the KS2 Basic guide.
