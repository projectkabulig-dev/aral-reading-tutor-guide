The parts of a session

Minutes and order can change by week. Always read the table.

1Opening Routine and Socio-Emotional Warm-Up
2Listening and Language Development through Storytelling
3Letter and Word Review from Previous Sessions
4Explicit Phonics and Word Work Instruction
5Fluency and Comprehension through Play-Based Practice
6Sight Word Recognition and Fluency Practice
7Closing / Reflection
Parts 1 and 2

Warm up, then listen

Example: Key Stage 2, Week 1

Feelings check-inLearners show how they feel with emoji cards
Energizer or songA short stretch, or a song with movements
Listening and languagePictures, a short video or questions, before a story
Parts 3 and 4

Review, then word work

Example: Key Stage 2, Week 1

ReviewLetters and words from earlier sessions
Introduce the new letterSay its sound. Learners say it and write it in the air.
Learners answer the LASFor example, circle the pictures that begin with the letter
Parts 5 and 6

Practice through play

Example: Key Stage 2, Week 1

Pick a picture cardSay what it is and its first sound
Match the letter cardPraise the learner. Take turns.
Blend syllables/mi/ + /sa/ = misa
Part 7

Closing and reflection

Think about what was learned.

TutorWhat did we learn today?
TutorWhat was the happiest part? Which part was hardest, and which was easiest?
TutorWhat helped us read better?

Learners may answer with emojis.

Added rule

A tutor is not a classroom teacher

✕Not this

  • Lecturing while learners do the LAS
  • Giving the answer when a learner hesitates

✓This

  • Learners answer the LAS and practice on their own
  • Help only when stuck: a question or a hint
Added rule

Match the guide to the learner

  • Check the Reading Profile: Phil-IRI and what you observe.
  • Too hard? A Key Stage 1 guide may fit better.
  • Strong? Move up to the Plus guide.
